MILECOUNT PARTNERSHIPS

Turn freight availability into better carrier decisions.

MileCount is seeking authorized data and pilot partners for a limited technical integration focused initially on cargo vans, Sprinters and box trucks.

What MileCount does

Normalizes available freight, checks vehicle fit, evaluates route/detour impact, applies carrier-specific economics and surfaces compatible trip opportunities.

What we're seeking

Authorized freight API/feed access, broker/dispatcher pilot datasets, technical integration partners, and carrier groups interested in controlled beta testing.

What we're not asking for

MileCount does not need to replace a partner's booking workflow. A provider can remain the source of truth for load status and booking while MileCount serves as an optimization/decision layer, subject to the provider's agreement and data terms.

Integration model

Provider feed → MileCount normalization → vehicle/capacity filter → route engine → economics → AutoStack recommendation → permitted booking/handoff workflow.

Pre-launch status

Functional MVP and backend infrastructure are built. Live automated freight remains disabled until authorized provider access is established.
