MileCount

A tool by Edit All Futures LLC.

Freight optimization software for independent commercial-vehicle operators.

Mission

Help carriers evaluate the economics of the entire trip—capacity, route, deadhead, fuel and operating costs—not only the headline loaded rate.

Initial users

Cargo van, Sprinter/high-roof van and 16–26 ft box-truck operators.

Business model

Subscription software for freight planning. Paid checkout is not yet active. See the billing page for current availability; future partner and fleet offerings will be announced when available.

Compliance posture

The current product is designed as decision-support software. It does not represent that MileCount is a freight broker or that automated demo freight is bookable. Any future activity involving arranging regulated transportation for compensation would be evaluated separately for applicable authority, contracts and compliance requirements.
