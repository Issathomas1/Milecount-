MileCount is building a freight decision layer for cargo vans, Sprinters and box trucks. It combines vehicle capacity, route economics and freight opportunities to help independent carriers evaluate more of every mile.

The problem

Deadhead

Loaded RPM can hide unpaid positioning and return miles.

Unused space

Vehicles can move with capacity that isn't generating revenue.

Fragmented decisions

Drivers compare rates, routes, fuel and costs across separate tools.

What MileCount evaluates

Vehicle fit

Payload, cargo space and vehicle class.

Route impact

Road miles, detours and home direction.

Carrier economics

Fuel, operating costs, break-even and all-miles RPM.

Trip combinations

AutoStack prototype evaluates compatible opportunities around an existing route.

Current status

Functional SaaS MVP with authenticated accounts, cloud vehicle/trip storage, manual real-load analysis, route economics, AutoStack proof of concept and subscription infrastructure. Automated live freight requires authorized provider integration.

MileCount outputs are planning estimates. Freight shown in automated demo flows may be simulated and is not bookable unless explicitly identified otherwise.
