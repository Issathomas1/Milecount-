MileCount Privacy Notice

Last updated October 9, 2026.

MileCount uses account, vehicle, trip and load-analysis information to provide route and freight-economics tools. Account authentication and application data are stored using Supabase. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe; MileCount does not store full payment-card numbers.

Information we use

Account identifiers, saved vehicle specifications, operating-cost assumptions, routes, analyzed loads, trip economics, subscription status and basic application events.

Why we use it

To authenticate users, save vehicles and trips, calculate economics, operate paid features, secure the service, troubleshoot errors and improve MileCount.

Sharing

We may use service providers needed to operate MileCount, including hosting/database, payment and future authorized freight-data providers. We do not state that freight providers or partners can access a user's private MileCount account data unless an integration specifically requires and discloses it.

Previous work-account connections

The car and package workspace was retired on October 9, 2026. New work-account connections and screenshot imports are no longer offered in the app. The information below describes data from previous use.

When you choose to connect a work account through Argyle, you authorize Argyle to retrieve the data described in its connection flow and share delivery activity and earnings with MileCount. Driver credentials are entered in Argyle’s interface; MileCount does not receive or store your driver password. Argyle processes your connection under its own privacy notice and terms.

MileCount uses recorded earnings, delivery dates, duration, mileage and connection status to show your completed work. Its backend stores a mapping between your MileCount account and Argyle user ID, the environment, consent date/version and request-control metadata. The earnings view is held in request and page memory, not saved to browser storage or a MileCount earnings database. Data freshness and available fields depend on the source platform.

Contact MileCount for assistance deleting a previous Argyle account connection and its associated shared records. This does not delete your original driver account or cancel deliveries. MileCount retains its account mapping and consent metadata until account-data deletion; contact MileCount for assistance with that metadata or connections you cannot access. Argyle privacy notice.

Offer screenshot importing

Optional screenshot recognition runs in your browser using Tesseract.js. The reader and English language files download from jsDelivr; that service receives ordinary download connection information, but MileCount does not upload the selected screenshot to it. Screenshots and raw recognized text stay in page memory and are cleared when you clear or use the import or leave the page. Reviewed offer fields are saved on your device under your MileCount account when copied into the offer form, like other offer drafts. Planning sends entered locations to the routing services used by MileCount.

Retention and choices

Data may be retained while an account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, billing, legal and operational purposes. Users may contact MileCount to request account-data assistance. Additional deletion/export controls are planned before broad public launch.

Estimates

Trip, fuel, RPM, margin and load-fit outputs are planning estimates and may depend on user-entered or third-party data.
