# MileCount Early Integration Outreach

## TrukTek
Subject: MileCount x TrukTek — authorized Van freight integration pilot

MileCount is a pre-launch carrier optimization SaaS for cargo vans, Sprinters and box trucks. A driver adds a committed trip, MileCount calculates remaining capacity and schedule availability, and AutoStack ranks compatible freight that could fill unused space.

We would like written authorization and the appropriate API access/agreement to test TrukTek Van freight in a limited MileCount private beta. We will build and maintain the integration ourselves. MileCount would not scrape or bulk-download the platform, redistribute TrukTek's load board, or act as a freight broker. TrukTek would remain the freight-data source and permitted booking/contact workflow.

Our integration layer already supports normalization, duplicate protection, freshness, route-corridor filtering, vehicle capacity, scheduling and provider attribution.

Could you advise the appropriate integration/Enterprise path and whether sandbox or limited pilot access is available?
